# Paid Time Off Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Labs (fictional demonstration company)
**Version:** 1.0 | October 2026

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least 10 business days before planned leave. Managers respond within 3 business days. Unused PTO may carry over up to 5 days into the next calendar year. Sick leave is recorded separately.
