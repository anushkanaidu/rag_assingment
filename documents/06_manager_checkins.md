# Manager Check-In Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Labs (fictional demonstration company)
**Version:** 1.0 | October 2026

Employees have a weekly one-on-one meeting with their manager every Friday. The meeting covers workload, blockers, feedback, and goals. New hires also have a formal 30-Day Check-in and a 90-Day Review. Employees may request a different weekly meeting time with their manager.
