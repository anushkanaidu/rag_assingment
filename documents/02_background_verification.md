# Background Verification Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Labs (fictional demonstration company)
**Version:** 1.0 | October 2026

Background verification (BGV) must be completed before Day 1. US employees are screened through HireRight; UK employees through Credence. If verification is delayed, HR must approve any revised start date. Employees should never email identity documents to personal accounts.
