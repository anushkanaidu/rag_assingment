# Benefits and Enrollment Guide

**Organization:** Northstar Labs (fictional demonstration company)
**Version:** 1.0 | October 2026

Eligible full-time employees can enroll in medical, dental, and vision benefits within 30 calendar days of their start date. HR provides enrollment instructions during orientation. Changes outside the enrollment window require a qualifying life event. Benefit questions go to the Benefits team.
