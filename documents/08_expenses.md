# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Labs (fictional demonstration company)
**Version:** 1.0 | October 2026

Employees submit business expense claims within 30 calendar days of purchase, including itemized receipts. Managers approve eligible claims before Finance reimbursement. Personal purchases are not reimbursable. Expenses above $500 require written manager approval before the purchase.

*This synthetic policy is for classroom evaluation only; it is not a real employer policy.*
